This document supplements the STEP assembly model and highlights only the adjustments and precautions that are not immediately obvious from the model itself.
Illustrated assembly instructions are not provided. Please refer to Complete_Assembly.step for the overall assembly structure.

The following notes highlight the areas that require particular attention during assembly.

Completely remove the built-in support structures from P-01 Body and P-02 Film Claw Arm before assembly.
The performance of the film transport mechanism depends heavily on the shape and adjustment of the tip of M-02 Film Claw. Fine-tune it as necessary.
P-03 Lever must move freely without binding after assembly. If necessary, ream or drill the pivot hole slightly to ensure smooth movement.
P-06 Front Roller Gear and P-07 Rear Roller Gear are different parts. Installing them in the wrong positions will prevent the mechanism from operating correctly.
M-09 Roller Pressure Spring can be made from the handle wire of a 15 mm binder clip. Modify it as required.
The springs are installed under significant compression. Wear appropriate eye protection during assembly, as the springs may eject unexpectedly.
Spot welding is recommended for attaching M-12 Latch Stud and C-03 Film Pack Spring to M-11 Back Panel.
